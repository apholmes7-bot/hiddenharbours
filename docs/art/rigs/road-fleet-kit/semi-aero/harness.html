Aero Sleeper Semi — harness

Standalone: no build, no deps, no project. Bakes any facing, paint, preset, part and pose off the same aeroSemiIsoRig.js the engine ships, and asserts the bake against aeroSemi.contract.json and the geometry in aeroSemiIsoRig.aeroSemi.gameplay.json — including the COUPLING group: the fifth wheel anchor, the plate top, and the 4 mm jackknife margin the pack's trailers rely on. Served from a folder it also re-hashes the rig against the contract's rigSha256; opened straight off disk the JSON fetches are blocked and those groups are skipped, not silently passed.

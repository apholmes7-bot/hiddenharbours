What pass 3 got wrong, and what changed
ALight was baked. Every sprite was lit once from the authored key, so a tree could not follow the hour and the terrain relight had to hold sprites to one band. The rig now emits light maps and is lit on call from a sky.
BPopcorn crowns. Each floret was lit from its own normal, so a crown had no light side. Normals now blend leaf, floret and crown envelope (an ellipsoid for broadleaves, a cone for conifers); one normal per stamp keeps one band per stamp.
CNo volume, no shadow. Sky visibility and sun transmission come from opacity maps over the real clumps and limbs: interiors, the lower crown under the upper, spruce tiers, the bole under an oak. The same map throws the cast shadow, with sun flecks and a canopy shade that stays under overcast.
DA fixed warm fringe on every crown's upper right. Gone; a rim appears only when the sun is behind the tree, on the silhouette edge that faces it, and thin foliage transmits.
ESway was a row shear. Four frames of sliding scanlines stair-stepped the trunk and dragged the crown as one sheet. Wind is now a field: trunk lean and sway, limb sway and bob per mass, flutter. Masses move whole pixels with their stamps; wood re-rasterises bent. Sixteen frames, looping.
FLeaves never moved. Deciduous stamps now turn over on their own cycles and show a paler underside, and the lit ones glint. Aspen trembles in still air, then birch, maple and oak, each stronger with wind.
GShapes. Florets were rosettes of equal satellites; now domed on top and flat underneath. White pine pom-poms became flat layered sprays swept up at the tip. Birch and aspen crowns close into one oval. Limbs curve through their elbows. Birch bark is warm white, not blue.
HTrees floated. Contact shade at the trunk foot, canopy shade under the crown, and the cast shadow, all on the pixel terrain and relit with it.
Engine contract
Per frame of a 16-frame wind loop, baked per wind level (calm · breeze · wind · gale), four maps on one cell and pivot:
_unlit
Structure bands under a flat sky: stamps, the dark between leaves, crevices, bark grain. No sun in it.
_normal
R x · G y-up · B toward camera. Stamp-flat, so per-pixel N·L lights a stamp as one unit.
_light
R sky visibility · G leaf thinness × translucency · B height above ground (0 = the foot, 255 = tree height).
_detail
RG stamp id + 1 (16 bit; 0 = between leaves) · B 255 leaf · 170 bark · 85 twig. A shader draws a stamp's seam on the side away from the sun and its tip toward it.
The reference relight is TreeRig4.relight(frame, sky); WeatherSky.at() is the sky, WeatherSky.lightTile() relights the terrain pack under the same sky, and toHarmony() feeds the harmony compositor. Cast shadows: TreeRig4.castShadow() gives exact ground levels per sky; engines that skew a flat shadow sprite read sky.shear. The sun path is a stage path: east at dawn, near the zenith before noon, west-northwest at sunset, so shadows fall toward the camera as in the harmony scenes. The 14:00 key is the one every rig was authored against.

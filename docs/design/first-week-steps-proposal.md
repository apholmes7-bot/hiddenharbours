# First-week steps proposal

Keep the whole first week on St Peters. The player stays with Ginny, earns money from shellfish, learns the rod from shore, buys Basil's broken dory, mends and launches her, and fishes from her. On day 7 or later, five facts make him ready to seek Ginny's word. The demo ends when she completes that evening word. The first-week notes end there, with no crossing or following-morning task. Full-game passage is a separate continuation.

This is a proposal for the Notebook TASKS page, not a change to the opening. It follows the [merged first-week canon](../vision-and-pillars.md) and [ADR 0062](../adr/0062-the-first-week-is-closed-by-weather.md), the owner's island-only direction, and the [five-fact proposal, PR 1511](https://github.com/apholmes7-bot/hiddenharbours/pull/1511). The [current-step characterisation, PR 1499](https://github.com/apholmes7-bot/hiddenharbours/pull/1499) records what the rewrite will replace. The canon is already on pinned main `f808af6ae5d5a0041f3d2147d74b1ddef7dce609`; later settled decisions control the demo endpoint. The [single source for Ginny's word](https://github.com/apholmes7-bot/hiddenharbours/blob/4b2d7f62f5d05f4b523c9f333fc3d1928914f472/docs/design/first-week-gate-word.md) owns her exact lines and completion contract. This page authors no alternate ending.

No HUD banners, forced errands, automatic goods transfers or invisible purchase rewards. The notes name a place or a piece of work; the player carries bagged purchases in the paper bag, larger gear in his hands, and every catch in his hands or a container. Each shelf item can run out independently. The island store has basic fishing supplies and repair materials once chosen; boat hardware is not sold on the island. A missing licence never prevents a cast, dig or trap haul: the unlicensed catch goes back, with the named explanation when he is ashore and no catch-record entry. Nobody speaks at the helm, including inner voice; releasing the catch never waits for speech. Do not queue the explanation for later playback.

## What counts, and what is only guidance

The gate has exactly these five requirements, read for the addressed player. The suggested teaching order below adds no sixth requirement. Day seven starts at absolute clock index 6, not after seven completed days; the word still waits for an evening talk.

| Requirement | Evidence | Missing TASKS text key in the fact proposal |
|---|---|---|
| The dory is mended | Existing `SaveData.RepairedBoats` contains the configured dory id, presently `boat.dory` | `tasks.st_peters.mend_dory` |
| He holds the cod licence | Existing `SaveData.OwnedLicenses` contains the configured licence id, presently `license.cod` | `tasks.st_peters.cod_licence` |
| He landed a kept fish from the dory | Fact proposal: `st_peters.fact.fish_from_dory` | `tasks.st_peters.fish_from_dory` |
| He sold shellfish | Fact proposal: `st_peters.fact.shellfish_sold` | `tasks.st_peters.sell_shellfish` |
| It is day 7 or later | Restore the Core clock normally, then pass `IGameClock.DayIndex` to `Read(save, dayIndex)`; index 6 begins day 7 | `tasks.st_peters.first_week` |

`IslandWeekRulesDef` in the fact proposal authors the threshold, licence, hull, finfish categories and text keys. `IIslandWeekProgress.Read(SaveData, int dayIndex)` at PR 1511 head `f6006e9fb9` returns held facts, ordered missing keys and `AllHold`; it sets nothing. Its caller passes the restored/live Core clock's zero-based absolute day index. It accepts neither seconds nor day length, and has no `ReadSaved` entry point. Do not add another time conversion or use the saved day cache or the day within a season. The separate recorder in that draft writes its two historical keys through the existing `OnboardingFlags` list; that describes the draft, not permission for additional persistence here. Save format remains version 26. Main now preserves shelf purchases and paper-bag contents on the existing rows; this proposal adds no saved field.

The fish fact means a freshly kept, licensed, non-protected finfish landed while the player occupies the configured dory and that hull is already repaired. `FreshCatchKept` captures hull identity and repair state before delivery callbacks. A shore or pier fish later carried aboard is not one, even when the notebook has forgotten its hand history. Another hull, a catch before repair, failed delivery and a released fish do not qualify. A shellfish sale must include an actual shellfish unit sold; selling cod, dumping spoiled clams or merely receiving money is not enough. The existing generic `landed_first_catch` flag cannot prove any of these distinctions.

The ordered notes are guidance. He can buy the rod before selling clams, get his licence before buying the boat, or meet all five facts without taking every lesson. Once all five hold, the next useful note is to see Ginny, even if an optional lesson is unfinished. The player never has to repeat a completed action just to perform it in the suggested order. Actual missing licences, supplies or repair work still matter to their own interactions.

All displayed alternatives fit the existing 31-character step budget. The first week should not appear as sixteen simultaneous chores. Arrival and welcome are context; the book shows the next useful note and retains meaningful completed work. Optional lessons must not become hidden eligibility requirements. Post-week travel is not a row in this sequence. If all five facts already hold, select the Ginny note even when earlier lessons are unfinished.

The rod/licence order is deliberate: a new player can get a licence before the shore lesson and retain a permitted catch. This does not forbid fishing earlier. The shore species offered in that lesson must match the accepted licence coverage, which is still a content decision. The notes must never suggest that a bare rod lets him retain every species.

Current source evidence already supplies a clam route: the [Shellfish Licence](../../Assets/_Project/Data/Resources/Catalog/Licenses/ClamLicense.asset) is sold by `seller.leblancs` and permits soft-shell clam, blue mussel and American oyster. Ginny fronts the fee; the licence must still be signed for. The St Peters general-store counter is wired to the island fish buyer. Moving this paperwork to the post office is a future choice, not the present route, and this source trace is not a playthrough.

The logbook/letter remains a separate personal thread. Suggested lines: “Read the letter he left” or “Take a quiet look at the letter”; completion remains `read_logbook`. Its predecessor is reaching the house, its successor is whichever island work remains. He can read it at any point. It is not a sixth gate fact, and the dory must not be described as inherited.

## What the rewrite still has to build

The later rewrite needs a read-only presentation adapter for these predicates, or an accepted extension to the quest condition model. It must keep one source of truth; do not save duplicate “step done” booleans for facts that already have a source. A text key is also not a settable gameplay flag.

The separate fact proposal records only the dory fish and shellfish sale. Any additional history described on this page must remain session-only with a plain snapshot until its persistence step is separately accepted; existing saved evidence may still be read. Reusing the flag list would still add saved meaning and is not authorised by this page. It does not supply exact dig, shore-fishing, first-row, guest-stay, repair-job or word histories. Proposed keys in this page are suggestions, not registrations. No mandatory saved checkbox should ship for a missing producer. An optional lesson can remain session guidance until its event and persistence rule are ready.

The three-job repair plan is accepted; its final material rows, quantities, prices and completion events still need implementation before the supplies and job notes can claim completion. A future save step would need agreed keys and producers for guest stay, exact digging, shore fishing, repair jobs, rowing, the word, plus per-player attribution, restore semantics and old-save tests. Repair quantities and the pending morning need an authoritative representation before promising reload continuity. Until that step is accepted, keep new progress in plain session snapshots. This page grants no saved field or new flag writer. The physical seller's dory also needs an agreed identity: the fact proposal uses main's `boat.dory`, which does not distinguish the new broken boat from the development dory.

Main now has four island sea-edge barriers (#1494). They stand only while `st_peters.first_week` is true and `st_peters.weather_turned` is false; their activation is dormant until a later producer sets the first flag. `CampaignBarrierDirector.Stands` consumes the weather-open flag immediately. Completing the evening word must therefore write neither passage-open state nor a substitute for it. The walking-bar surf and demo termination still need their own integration. Sea-edge assets alone do not establish a closed, playable island week.

Ginny's conversation follows the exact ending and completion rules in #1533. The demo ends at her evening word and has its own save file; there is no import into the full game promised by this page. Full-game morning passage is outside these first-week notes. Its timing, sleep, reload and weather rules remain in that single source. No part of that transition belongs in the read-only fact evaluator.

## One book for each player

The Notebook has one **CAUGHT** list and no **FREED** page. Unlicensed releases make no CAUGHT entry; kept catch records and the fresh-dory fact remain different evidence. Pinned main currently uses `NotebookContent.LabelCaught = "CATCH"`; the settled copy is `CAUGHT`, replacing that label in a later text change without creating a second list. This proposal changes no runtime label.

The player is male: narration says "the player" or "he". The demo supports two to four players. Notes, optional lesson history, conversation selection and completion are personal. Use an explicit player identity and that player's facts; no singleton, pooled caught list or host's licence may silently supply another player's requirements. The current recorder wiring is not proof of multiplayer attribution.

As #1533 specifies, each player's completed word ends his own playable demo; others retain their unfinished progress. A shared closing presentation follows completion by its participants. New presentation history and ending state remain plain per-player session snapshots. A later save step needs stable player attribution, durable completion, isolated demo-file selection, exact restore/reconnect semantics and old-save cases. It must not reopen a completed demo or overwrite full-game progress. No saved field or flag writer is added here.

Before shipping the rewrite, migrate the pinned old-step tests to the accepted new rules with their reasons, retaining assertions for exact next note, completion source, out-of-order actions and older saves. Recheck the actual catch/sale listeners as well as the pure fact table. Paper-bag carrying, separate shelf stock, repair-by-hand and the weather boundary each need their own integration proof; this document neither changes nor claims visual proof of them.

## Remaining content and compatibility work

- The first cod licence must be sold on the island. The exact counter and full shore-species coverage remain content dependencies; the note names only St Peters.
- Ginny fronts the shellfish-licence fee for the player to sign. One-time shortfall support is accepted once it cannot charge twice. This page introduces no debt, new charge or repayment interaction.
- Three hand jobs are accepted. Material rows, quantities and prices must support actual carrying and work at the slip; no note invents delivery or completed repair history.
- Four rods at 60 on the island shelf are accepted. Counted stock and the physical purchase point remain separate implementation. Borrowing a rod can satisfy an optional lesson without forcing a duplicate purchase; purchase evidence remains separate from possession.
- Preserve existing access for older saves through an explicit compatibility policy before applying new weather restrictions. Never manufacture either historical fact from `onboarded`, money or an already-boarded hull.
- The exact evening word, first eligible evening encounter and demo endpoint are settled by #1533. The separate demo save is settled; cross-edition transfer is not promised.

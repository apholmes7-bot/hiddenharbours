Unzip everything, open the game folder and double-click Red Skies At Night.exe.
Keys: WASD or arrows move; Shift sprints; E interacts; Esc pauses or cancels; use the mouse for menus. At the helm, W/S changes throttle, A/D steers and Space braces.
Save and Player.log: %USERPROFILE%/AppData/LocalLow/One More Minute Games/Red Skies At Night/ (savegame.json is the save).

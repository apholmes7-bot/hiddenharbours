# Living fish: decisions for later review

These are design choices for a later review, not questions sent during this run. The recommendations are the working defaults for the proposal. Existing rules already settle manual transport, deterministic unsaved background schools, supported bodies, no gore and no shader edits in this charter; they are not reopened here.

| Choice | Options in plain words | Recommendation and reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1. How noticeable should landed movement be? | Short, quiet tail movements; more frequent whole-body flops; always quiet after landing. | Short, quiet movement, with an immediate quiet-presentation option. It adds life without requiring the player to watch distress. |
| 2. Should alive catch sell for more? | Same price; a small shellfish-only premium; a premium for all live catch. | Same price in the first handling slice. Freshness already rewards good handling; a new premium needs economy balancing. |
| 3. What can the first bucket keep alive? | Suitable shellfish only; finfish too; ordinary carrying only. | Suitable shellfish only, matching the existing live-storage rule. Finfish need real water/oxygen/capacity data before a livewell is promised. |
| 4. How soon should the new field appear in play? | One tested slice after habitat/consumer wiring; every region at once; keep as design for now. | One tested slice after wiring. Current data defaults stay disabled until all existing school readers agree. |
| 5. How should a school travel between habitats? | Start with safe local patrols; add connected daily/tidal routes immediately; roam freely across the map. | Safe local patrols first, then connected routes in M2. Crossing unknown or dry ground to imitate migration would damage realism. |
| 6. Which creatures get the next individual treatment? | Existing finfish; lobster/crab; clams/mussels/oysters. | Existing finfish first, then the regional bed creatures with support data. Reuse the current catch/fight vocabulary and never draw a swimming clam. |
| 7. How urgent should putting fish away feel? | The proposed short movement and gentle alive budget; a longer alive budget; no visible alive timer. | The proposed short movement with no HUD death countdown. Freshness and visible storage already make handling legible; tune budgets during playtest. |
| 8. How physical should the underwater effect become? | Keep current tinted sprites; share water transmission/refraction when the water lane is ready; add full fish shadows and feeding displacement immediately. | Share transmission/refraction after the water owner defines the path. Retain today's presentation until approved comparisons and a laptop GPU profile exist. |

No choice here authorizes a save-format, ground, art or renderer change. The backlog assigns those later slices to their owning roles.

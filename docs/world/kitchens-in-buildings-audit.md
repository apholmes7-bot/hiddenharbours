# Kitchens in buildings audit

Audit baseline: `f5be0efa36eea29a5e93f5cce6212e7af1fa9142` (main snapshot, 2026-11-15). This is a read-only inventory and implementation proposal. No scene, prefab, asset, routine, save format or game code changes accompany it. It serves P3 (a living working coast), P4 (food and furniture carried by hand) and P5 (a useful home).

## 1. What exists on main

**There are 10 placed building interiors: seven homes and three workplaces, all in St Peters. All 10 need kitchen or meal facilities; all 10 require a world-content/art editor pass to add and verify the missing visible pieces.** This is a count of buildings, not room sheets, floors or future property deeds. Four buildings have an upper floor, giving 14 placed floor layers. The placements below are proposals from serialized dimensions and authored furnishing plans, not a visual acceptance or a clearance proof.

The inventory follows `BuildingInterior` script references through GameObject/Transform parents in every tracked scene and prefab, then checks room contracts, builder tables and all 14 routine assets. No building prefab contains a stood `BuildingInterior`. In addition to St Peters' 10 components, Nine Mile Creek has one on a **fuel-canopy see-through effect**, not an occupied room (`CreekFuel/Route91FuelStop/station.nmc_canopy/seeThrough`, [scene](../../Assets/_Project/Scenes/NineMileCreek.unity#L382864)); it is excluded. Cumberland, Rice Point, Greybox, Greywick, EastWater and SouthWater contain no stood building interiors. SampleScene and the URP scene template contain none either. Boats are outside this audit.

There are also **four unplaced room offerings**: restaurant and fish-market ground floors in the shop contract, plus the station C-store and pay-booth sales floors in `StationPieceDef` assets. They are listed separately below. They are not four more playable building interiors. The current station key-scene shell is not proof that the older station builder's room has been opened.

## 2. Kitchen pieces and proposed locations

### Rules used for the recommendations

Every home gets a pantry, one cold-storage choice, a food prep surface, cooktop, table and seats. A workplace staffed across lunch gets the same compact provision, with a shared clean staff area rather than food on a working counter. The small pay booth is the exception: packed food, cold storage and a seat suffice; hot food comes from the C-store or home. A commercial fish cooler is not automatically the staff's lunch container.

Use an **ice-fed cooler** for the small island homes and packed-lunch workplaces first: it makes replenishment and carrying ice explicit without inventing electricity availability. Propose a **fridge** at the general store, restaurant and C-store; its supply/operating requirements must come from the shared kitchen rules, not an assumed power grid. A cooler is the fallback where those rules do not support a fridge yet. These are choices of facility, not new spoilage balance values.

Existing tables and chairs in village furnishing arrays are scenery with colliders, not dining or sitting interactables. `InteriorFixture` currently dispatches stairs, beds and wardrobes only ([enum](../../Assets/_Project/Code/App/Editor/StPetersInteriors.cs#L195), [fixture dispatch](../../Assets/_Project/Code/App/Editor/StPetersInteriors.cs#L802)). Likewise, a drawn hearth, potbelly, ice chest, larder or prep table is **not proof of cooking, cold storage or an inventory**. Village ground sheets declare a hearth in their [bake options](../../Assets/_Project/Art/Sprites/Interiors/Interiors.json#L19); none declares a functional cooktop. The shared kitchen task must supply pantry/cooler/fridge, seated dining and limited sitting-rest semantics before these become interactions. Sitting must remain weaker than sleeping; this audit sets no recovery numbers.

Locations below are **door-relative zones on the ground floor**: front is the wall with the actual entrance; left/right are as a person entering sees them. They are not compass directions or world coordinates. Final anchors must use each room's `InteriorFootprint`, facing and actual door sign/offset ([coordinate conversion](../../Assets/_Project/Code/World/InteriorFootprint.cs#L111)). Preserve the existing clear threshold lane, hearth access, stair landing and access to each seat. The furnishing table deliberately reserves the doorway lane ([constraint](../../Assets/_Project/Code/App/Editor/StPetersInteriors.cs#L435)). A new visible kitchen piece or changed baked fixture belongs to a content/art lane with editor clearance and visual proof; these desk proposals authorize no re-bake.

### Proposed player purchase and placement path

**Use fixed authored spots for the first existing homes/workplaces; use player-selected grid cells inside a validated room for later custom buildings on owned parcels.** This preserves the current hand-authored circulation while fulfilling the direction that a player can arrange the kitchen in their own building. “Free placement” should mean choosing legal cells/quarter-turns on the decor grid, not unrestricted continuous positioning through walls. The exact grid step and prop footprint come from data; do not equate a farm cell with a floor cell.

1. **Define and sell actual pieces.** Propose one stable definition per pantry, cooler, fridge, prep unit, cooktop, table and seat, with price, carry package, footprint, supported facing, fixture role and any compatible slot tags. These are proposed definitions, not existing classes. Put pricing/offers with Economy content and a merchant's wares book. The existing [BuyCatalog stock/dispatch](../../Assets/_Project/Code/Economy/BuyCatalog.cs#L134) and [BuyKind](../../Assets/_Project/Code/Economy/BuyLogic.cs#L4) would need an explicit furniture extension; do not disguise an appliance as an ice supply or fit it to a boat through an instrument purchase.
2. **Collect, carry, put down.** Paying leaves a furniture package at the merchant's pickup point until collected. Carry it home in the player's hands or by a supported transport method, then unpack at a legal floor position. Reuse the Core [ICarriable](../../Assets/_Project/Code/Core/Interaction/ICarriable.cs#L32), [IInteractable](../../Assets/_Project/Code/Core/Interaction/IInteractable.cs) and [HandSlots](../../Assets/_Project/Code/Core/Interaction/HandSlots.cs#L110) seams, with an explicit furniture load classification/carry capability if needed. Current hands are tuned to tools, containers and catch, so furniture is an extension, not a free existing capability. Large furniture waits for a supported carrying/hauling solution; purchase never teleports it into a house. Food and ice likewise enter/leave each storage by hand, with visible stock supplied by the content lane.
3. **Validate permission and circulation through Core.** A proposed property/placement service must distinguish an owned home from a purchased land parcel and expose a permission query. Fixed spots refer to a building **instance**, floor and stable slot id. Custom-building placement checks the room's transformed footprint, wall band, doors, stairs, other props, floor support and walkable approaches to every seat/work face. The existing [InteriorFootprint](../../Assets/_Project/Code/World/InteriorFootprint.cs) supplies geometry; it is not by itself a placement service. Floor validity and ownership must be checked again when the player commits placement.
4. **Attach the shared rules.** Prep and cooktop pieces use the existing recipe station ids [`station.prep` and `station.cooktop`](../../Assets/_Project/Code/Core/Food/RecipeStations.cs#L10). A house or boat should adapt to the same Core kitchen/storage/seat contracts when that separate task lands, rather than World referring to a Boats implementation. A table claims explicit adjacent seats; a seated meal applies the shared dining bonus, and sitting-rest uses its capped rule. The current [INeedsService](../../Assets/_Project/Code/Core/Needs/INeedsService.cs#L16) exposes eating and sleeping, not a sitting operation; sleeping cannot be reused as the chair action.
5. **Persist instances, not pictures.** A future layout record needs a stable furniture-instance id, piece definition id, property/building-instance id, region, floor, legal slot or grid cell, rotation, and references to that container's contents/ice state. Recover contents and identity when furniture moves; moving a stocked cooler cannot duplicate or reset it. This needs an explicitly designed save addition/migration; current [SaveData](../../Assets/_Project/Code/Core/Save/SaveData.cs) and `HomeDeeds` are not a furniture-layout store. Recompute collision/visuals from accepted definitions. This audit makes no save changes.

The first implementation can author fixed kitchens, then prove the shared kitchen interactions at those spots. Player furniture purchases/placement follow as their own reviewed slice; custom land construction follows the existing parcel/construction dependency. Acceptance should cover refused/unowned placements without charging or losing an item, occupied/unsupported floor cells, accessible seats, manual transfers, save/load without duplicate furniture or contents, and deterministic rule behavior. Visual placement/facing/clearance still needs the relevant content lane's editor proof.

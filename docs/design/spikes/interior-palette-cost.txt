INTERIOR PALETTE — THE COST OF THE TWO DESIGNS (full mesh interiors, PR 1)
Both arms generated from the shipped facet shader by targeted transforms.
Unity 6000.5.0f1 · Direct3D12 · NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060

1. UNIFORM COST — exact, not sampled. A float4[N] is 16N bytes of constant buffer.

   arm      _RampMeta   interior table   ramp uniform bytes   delta vs shipped
   --------------------------------------------------------------------------
   BASE          16            -            256                   0
   WIDEN        48            -            768                +512  (ALL hulls, always)
   SCOPED off    16            -            256                   0  (byte-identical)
   SCOPED on     16          24            640                +384  (only while a cut is live)

   WIDEN is paid by every hull in every frame, because _RampMeta is outside the
   gate's #ifdef and every hull material carries it. SCOPED's extra table lives
   inside #ifdef HH_LEVEL_GATE, and IsoFacetHullRenderer.ApplyCutawayKeyword only
   enables that keyword while a cut is actually live — so on a harbour of boats
   nobody is inside, SCOPED costs literally nothing and WIDEN costs 512 B each.

4. THE SUB-MESH VARIANT OF CANDIDATE (b), for completeness.

   Scoping the second table by MATERIAL SLOT instead of by the interior tag costs
   one extra draw per hull that has interior geometry visible, and splits the hull
   into two sub-meshes. Structural, not sampled: every committed hull mesh is
   subMeshCount = 1 today (measured by the S0 spike, 24/24), and IsoFacetHullRenderer
   draws one material per hull. It is strictly worse than selecting on a tag the
   vertex data already carries, so it is reported rather than built.

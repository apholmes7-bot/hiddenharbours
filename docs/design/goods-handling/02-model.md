# Goods handling model

**Proposed design, no game code or balance change.** Authority: [canon](../../vision-and-pillars.md) P2/P4/P5, the 2026-11-10 handling ruling, and [existing physical inventory §4](../diegetic-ui-and-inventory.md#4-physical-limited-inventory). Every move follows a visible supported route: source → hand or lifting device → destination. Walking near a boat, shop or truck never transfers ownership. A counter may purchase only goods physically presented there.

## Hands and sizes

Keep the existing two-hand sharing rule and continuity law: a pickup fills a free hand and never silently displaces the other item. One loose catch at a time; a quantity of clams is carried inside a container, not as an unlimited hand stack. A small catch plus a pail, or a pail plus a tool, can share the pair. A two-hand load reserves the pair for one entity. No extra cargo slot is created by putting a tool on a sling.

Store an item's actual caught weight and size envelope; resolve its handling class from those and authored profiles. These **proposed** classes describe required hands, not inventory counts:

| Class | Physical example | Carry and fit rule |
|---|---|---|
| `small_piece` | One clam or banded lobster, tiny fish | One catch hand; individual dimensions still required. No stack in the hand. |
| `one_hand_fish` | Fish at or below the existing 0.62 m length default | One catch hand if the other occupant is a permitted different kind. New mass ceiling is a proposed Def dial, not a change shipped here. |
| `two_hand_fish` | A fish longer than that threshold but within the two-hand envelope | Both hands free; no displaced rod. A proposed **1.15 m / 12 kg** upper envelope includes the current 12 kg cod (about 1.092 m under main's size curve). Validate every reachable M1 species maximum before cutover; oversized fish require a dimensionally compatible pan/device, not any small pan by name. |
| `one_hand_container` | Modestly filled pail | Bail grip, proposed gross hand limit 8 kg. Contents must fit the inner volume and opening. A few 0.25 m fish and many 0.10–0.15 m fish can be alternative loads; a 0.9 m cod cannot fit a pail. |
| `two_hand_container` | Light handled pan or small tote | Both hands and two authored grips; proposed gross limit 16 kg, also constrained by size. Cannot be filled while occupying both hands; set it down first. |
| `device_load` | Loaded lobster crate, large insulated tub, pallet | Refuse hand pickup. Requires compatible cart, fork pockets or lifting points. Two-person handling is later work; until implemented, a second person is not assumed to exist. |

Keep capacity **size based** as canon requires. Mass is only a lift/device limit, not a replacement for geometric fit or a new HUD weight meter. Existing one-hand length and species length-from-weight defaults remain until a separately reviewed rule migration; the proposed extra lift limits need gameplay-systems and economy-sim validation.

## Container and storage rules

Each container has a `ContainerDef`: outside dimensions and opening, usable inside shape, an occupancy grid/cell size or volume budget, allowed item profiles, content count ceiling if used, allowed child containers, empty and loaded stack compatibility/heights, handles/lift mode, gross limit, and permitted preservation modes. All limits live in data. An item's entire envelope must enter through the opening and fit inside; remaining litres alone never make a long fish fit.

The full target uses the canon's fullscreen container grid, with item footprints, allowed rotations and bounded layers; circular pails mask out corner cells. Depth is explicit, not an infinite stack behind a flat icon. Cells are an inspection/packing abstraction backed by metric envelopes and occupied volume. Small shellfish keep individual identity even when an art fill sprite depicts several. M1 uses authored placements and per-profile capacities derived from these dimensions, retaining the current count ceiling as an additional ceiling; it does not build a general packing editor/solver. M2 adds manual arrangement. A placement algorithm has stable ordering `(placementOrder, itemId)`, never a physics settle or unseeded shuffle.

Representative **game proposals**, not surveyed product specifications; internal dimensions must be checked by content validation. A rectangular figure below is usable clear space; pail is a cylinder. Sizes are metres. Gross lift limits above include shell, ice, water and contents. Fixed storage has reach/opening/support facts even though it cannot be lifted.

“Max 3” means three containers total, not a base plus three. A stack is a support chain of distinct container instances. A lower container cannot be opened, removed or moved independently while supporting others; move the supported stack only with a compatible device and gross/height checks. Nesting empties reduces envelope by a declared insertion depth and consumes an empty child instance; it creates no hidden usable inventory. No containment or support cycles. The smallest slice has no stacking or nesting UI.

First-slice fit must cover current legal catch sizes end to end. The 1.092 m cod travels directly in both hands to the long receiving pan; the proposed 0.60 m clear tote/pan is **not** its destination. gameplay-systems and economy-sim audit all M1 size maxima, and world-content/art-director verify the receiving site before enabling it. If another legal M1 catch cannot traverse the route, resolve its handling/profile/site with the owning roles before cutover; do not shrink the catch, delete stock, grant a stronger hand, or present an inaccessible sale as complete.

## Transfers and visible fill

A single authority validates source item, source/destination revision, player reach, current region/level/deck, opening, free hands, item compatibility, occupancy and support. One commit removes and inserts the **same id**. A refusal changes neither inventory nor money nor freshness. Selection and capacity preview reserve nothing until commit; a stale preview fails plainly. A blocked put leaves the item in hand.

Portable containers carry their contents; putting a pail on a cart moves the pail entity and changes its support, not every fish into a cart list. Fixed storage is tied to an authored location or boat instance. Deck support follows the boat's frame; wharf support uses its own height. Never infer elevation from sorting order, or put a container at ground height while drawing it over a bed.

Every open container shows sprites of its **actual** contents filling and emptying. Fill reads item identity, species, measured size, placement and freshness. Child containers are drawn as children occupying real space, with visible contents when open. A closed lid may occlude contents; opening it reveals the same inventory. No generic “full tote” sprite may remain after the last fish leaves. Representation can aggregate many small shells into an authored fill band, but cannot show species not present or claim extra capacity. Art-director supplies rim/interior/grip/support geometry; art-pipeline supplies fill sprites, occlusion and depth/sort integration. They must respect ADR 0053 for held props; this design does not change the game's look.

## Goods on floor or deck

Later M2 intentional set-downs may put a loose item on a verified dry supported surface, with region, level/deck, metric position and item id saved. No automatic full-container spill in M1. A pan's deliberate tip validates enough supported placements for **every** selected item before moving any; if no room, all stay in the pan. A floor item does not float at hand height, cross a railing, or disappear into a count. It remains recoverable and participates in obstruction rules without permanently blocking the player's route.

Life state and freshness are different. Proposed life state is `alive` / `dead`; spoil is the existing freshness accumulator. Living shellfish can retain life in a species-compatible wet/cool setting. On dry deck or floor they lose protected Live storage and use Ambient spoilage immediately; a species Def may also set a game-time survival deadline, after which life becomes dead. An authored dead-on-landing fish begins dead. Missing survival data disables live-floor placement rather than inventing a biological timer. Neither death nor a move resets accrued spoil. Freezing makes life dead, preserves the banked spoil and applies Frozen storage. No container revives a dead animal. Freshness does not itself prove life.

Use existing `Freshness.WithMode` at storage transitions and `SettleThroughProtection` across ice expiry. Live/Frozen/Iced zero-rate M1 defaults remain; the general economy document's slower-rate examples are future balance choices. Ice, lid, remaining cooling budget and melt-settle instant belong to the container and must persist before portable cold holding ships. Wet storage is species-specific: **not every shellfish belongs in a freshwater pail**. M1 retains its existing wet-bucket policy rather than silently adding mortality or changing balance.

Keep it non-graphic: a still fish, subdued movement for live shellfish, an existing freshness cue and a plain “no longer fresh” refusal. Detailed suffering, gore and disease are out. Art decisions go to the art roles; logic tests assert life/storage facts, never a new look.

## Save versus recompute

Save player actions and history: stable item/container instance ids, exact rolled weight and resolved size, parent/location, support reference, hand side/two-hand occupancy, content order/placement, freshness triple, life state/deadline when enabled, ice/cold state and monotonic id sequence. A saved live-world mutation cannot be reconstructed from seed and time alone. Save all sides of a transfer and wallet/market changes in one consistent snapshot; after reload an item has exactly one location.

Recompute Def lookup, accepted classes, physical fit, total mass/occupied space, display sprites/fill, sort/depth placement, current spoil and protection status at saved game time. Recompute tide/weather from `(worldSeed, gameTime, place)` through Core, never save their samples. Unresolved content/parents go to a deterministic visible recovery record retaining the original facts; do not drop the item, shrink it to species midpoint, or silently return it to a remote boat. [Data proposal](03-data-model-proposal.md) defines the handoff and migration boundaries.

# Atlantic landing buyers and receiving data

**Research and proposed game data, checked 2026-11-10.** Receiving needs separate facts for physical delivery, product weight, grade, preservation and payment. The table below draws on Canadian fisheries agencies, a Nova Scotia buyer's own process and Newfoundland cod agreements. The proposed fields translate those practices into the [manual goods design](https://github.com/apholmes7-bot/hiddenharbours/pull/1042); they create no Def assets, statutory rules, prices or runtime systems.

## Receiving operations and candidate data

Each row identifies its source's scope. A documented plant process is not a practice at every Atlantic wharf, and a historical agreement is not today's universal rule. Candidate ids are illustrative and unreserved. The game proposals in the last column are design inferences, separate from the documented operations.

| Receiving operation | Documented Atlantic or Canadian practice | Physical equipment | Candidate data the game can build |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing verification | DFO describes independent third-party verification of landed species and weight; coverage follows fishery/licence arrangements. Its buyer purchase-slip tool separately records species, weight, price and date. [DFO commercial requirements](https://www.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/fisheries-peches/sdc-cps/index-eng.html) | Landing/weighing station and receipt, distinct from grade inspection | `LandingRecord`: trip, species, measured mass, verification status and game time; `PurchaseReceipt`: delivered lot, buyer, accepted amount and payment. Do not make every buyer an independent monitor. |
| Product weight without added material | DFO's export catch-certification guidance excludes ice, brine and other additions from product weight. This source concerns reporting weights, not one mandated wharf tare procedure. [DFO weight guidance](https://fcs-scp.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/fcsweb/FCS_HELP/Standard_Weight_Guidance.htm) | Scale, receiving container and a specified drain/weigh method | `WeighingProfile`: gross, container/packaging tare, excluded ice/water and net product kg; method, units and provenance are explicit. Never pay for the tub or count ice as fish. |
| Cod boxes and tubs | The **2021/22** Newfoundland cod schedule documents slush-style grey boxes, layered fish/ice with drain plugs for dry icing, and a traceability form recording per-tub weight. It generally places sample grading at the processing plant. Historical equipment/workflow evidence, not current pricing terms. [Cod schedule, pp. 1–2 and 8–10](https://ffaw.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/2021-cod-schedule-.pdf) | Watertight fish box/tub for slush; draining box for layered ice; supported unloading | `ReceivingProfile`: species/product form, compatible tub Def, drain state, ice/water budget, lot id and grade location. Plugged slush and draining ice are different modes; both consume real capacity. |
| Cod grade and commercial adjustments | The May 6 **2026** agreement announcement retains differentiated cod grades and sampling. It describes a season-specific ice/water deduction **pending DFO agreement**, distinct from quota deduction. This is evidence that commercial terms vary, not authority to apply that deduction everywhere. [FFAW agreement announcement](https://ffaw.ca/%F0%9F%93%A3-2026-cod-price-agreement-reached/) | Sampling station, grade record and buyer receipt | `GradePolicy`: sampling/assessment stage and criteria revision; `CommercialAdjustmentPolicy`: separately named terms and basis. Do not use a universal percentage or confuse payable amount with verified landed mass. |
| Lobster crate intake | A Nova Scotia buyer describes unloading tagged crates, receiving/weighing/scanning at its plant or other wharves, then temporary tank holding. [Buyer receiving process](https://www.lobstersrus.ca/the-process) | Tagged reusable crates, scale, wharf/truck and holding tank | `buyer.receiving_live_lobster`: crate identity, source lot, handover time, container ownership and compatible live-holding route. Scanning records a delivered crate; it does not move one. |
| Lobster sorting and QA trays | That buyer grades individuals by weight and storability, checks weakness/death, softness, damage and culls, and uses QA trays linked to the originating crate. [Buyer sorting process](https://www.lobstersrus.ca/the-process) | Sorting table/line, individual scale and separate inspection trays | Separate `SizeBand`, `LifeRequirement`, `ConditionGrade` and `Disposition`. A QA tray is a physical destination, not a deletion queue; a live lobster is not automatically premium grade. |
| Lobster pans and holding trays | The same plant describes stacked pans and submerged trays supplied with filtered seawater; some long-term trays have individual compartments. [Buyer holding process](https://www.lobstersrus.ca/the-process) | Live-holding facility, pans/trays and supported racks | A later facility profile supplies the compatible habitat and compartment layout. A loose tray alone supplies no water, power or indefinite preservation. |
| Live lobster and ice compatibility | The Canadian industry council describes moist, cool holding and warns against freshwater exposure, including ice melt. This food-service guidance supports distinguishing habitats; it supplies no game mortality duration. [Lobster Council handling](https://lobstercouncilcanada.ca/lobster-101/handling-lobster/) | Compatible cool/live holding, separate from a generic iced-fish tub | Reference species/habitat compatibility. Do not turn every wet bucket into a lobster tank or infer life from an `Iced` freshness mode. |
| Live bivalve lot receiving | The Canadian Shellfish Sanitation Program specifies conditional container tagging or bulk transaction records, with species, harvest location/date and harvester identity. Receiving identification maintains the shellstock lot; traceability follows the supply chain. [CFIA manual §§9.3.3 and 11.3](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-guidance-commodity/fish/canadian-shellfish-sanitation-program) | Identified shellstock container and receiving-lot record | `ShellstockLot`: species, harvest area/date, source id and lot code. Preserve provenance when splitting or combining deliveries; a pail's contents do not become an anonymous global clam count. |

## Equipment fields required before authoring

| Equipment role | Data needed in its own Def or measured support contract | Receiving consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Reusable crate | Outside/inside envelope, opening, empty tare, grips, loaded lift limit, stack compatibility and ownership | Weigh contents separately; preserve crate id and return it through a real carry leg |
| Insulated fish tub | Tare, usable volume, payload, plug/drain modes, ice/water compatibility, lifting points/fork pockets and support limits | A full machine tub cannot become a hand-held tote; ice and water share its volume/mass budget |
| Sorting pan or QA tray | Measured inner envelope, shallow depth, supported surface, handling method and intended profile | A long fish must actually fit; inspection/refusal does not erase the animal or its freshness history |
| Live-holding tray/rack | Compartments, stack/support clearance and facility habitat dependency | Tray placement supplies no live protection without the compatible operating facility |
| Receiving scale/support | Accessible load surface, opening, gross load and allowed container/profile ids | No weighing while cargo remains remotely in a boat or truck; an oversized load needs another supported route |

Measured dimensions, local pan sizes and closed-lid stack ratings remain in the [handling research work](https://github.com/apholmes7-bot/hiddenharbours/pull/1050). The [RULED choices of 2026-11-11](../../backlog/proposed-goods-handling.md#ruled-handling-choices--2026-11-11) select a 0.65 × 0.45 m outside footprint for the low sorting pan and two-total compatible insulated-tote stacks; they do not supply measured tare, lid geometry or support ratings. The short sorting pan is distinct from the long counter receiving pan required to fit current large cod. Do not infer measurements, tare or machine capacity from a product name or photo. These equipment fields carry no sprite or rig prescription.

## Proposed data records

One entity per file with stable ids, following [the data model](../architecture/data-model.md). Reference existing species, container, commodity and config ids instead of copying their catalogs. Keep receiving shape and buyer policy separate so a new buyer does not require a new save field.

| Candidate Def or state | Minimum fields and units | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| `BuyerReceivingDef` | Stable id; allowed species/product forms and container ids; receiving support; hours/access; inspection stage; grade/weigh policy ids; accepted dispositions | Complete physical source → hands/device → receiving route for every accepted profile; no remote inventory source |
| `WeighingPolicyDef` | Method; mass unit; disjoint tare/addition categories; drain/settle rule where applicable; resolution and rounding; accepted receipt basis | Finite nonnegative masses, no overlap between deduction categories, net within observed gross, explicit handling of inconsistent measurements |
| `BuyerGradeDef` | Stable grade id; size-band policy; life requirement; condition criteria; sampling scope; refusal/disposition; revision | Independent life/size/quality facts; criteria and sampling stage supplied by data, no inferred universal lobster or cod bands |
| `ReceivingLotState` | Lot id, delivered entity ids, origin links, measured result, grade result/assessment time and ownership | Exact individual identities survive grouped display, splitting and partial acceptance |
| `SaleReceiptState` | Sale id, buyer/lot, accepted entities, price basis, disclosed adjustment terms, rounding result and game time | Consume goods and update wallet/market once; rejected/unconfirmed goods remain owned and retrievable |

The current economy prices catch by units/HU. A future mass-based buyer must receive its own reviewed pricing and rounding change; this table does not silently convert the existing market to kg pricing. Commercial grade, current freshness and current life remain separate. Real contamination and statutory compliance are outside this game's quality model; no freshness value certifies food safety.

## Weight and payment example

**Synthetic arithmetic fixture:** a supported load weighs 24 kg gross, comprising a 3 kg tub, 5 kg of separately measured ice/water, and 16 kg product. Its physical product weight is `24 − 3 − 5 = 16 kg`. Each component is disjoint; do not remove the same water again through a second automatic allowance. A particular contract adjustment, if later authored, belongs on the commercial receipt and is not a fabricated change to physical mass or official landing data.

The reusable tub remains a separate owned entity. Sorting the product into accepted and refused trays preserves the sum of product mass and the individual ids. Ice melting or a draining action settles the cold chain and material balance; a weighing operation does not restore quality. At confirmation, only selected accepted goods are consumed and only their current policy quote is paid.

## First playable receiving route and later expansion

Follow the ruled first-sale decision: first set one fish on a compatible counter support, inspect it, show the ordinary quote/refusal, confirm the sale and take back anything refused. This is a manual receiving interaction; it does not add official monitoring, laboratory work or a plant conveyor. Preserve today's price rules until a separate pricing change is approved.

M2 may accept a delivered pail or compatible tote, retain lot identity and return the empty container. Plant sampling, industrial tubs/live racks and [paid carrying](goods-carrying-staff-proposal.md) follow their M3/facility prerequisites; freight remains M4. Pending grade or measurement leaves the actual goods in receiving storage, never in a remote source pointer. The [future save contract](goods-persistence-proposal.md) must preserve that location, provenance, cooling and once-only settlement before this loop ships.

Acceptance uses synthetic receipts: empty-container tare is excluded; added materials are not fish; a full/closed or incompatible receiver refuses without mutation; life alone does not pass grade; partial acceptance conserves ids/mass; retry pays once; and a reusable container can be carried back. Local measurements, actual buyer terms and art acceptance are implementation inputs, not numbers invented here.

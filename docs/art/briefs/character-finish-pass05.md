# Character finish pass 05 — beauty and clarity

Owner request, 2026-09-13: charming characters with the craft expected of a premium release,
expressed through low-poly forms and pixel art. This extends CW-01 before production intake.
It serves P3: memorable people who belong to the working coast.

Owner correction: **"The eyes do not look good."** Pass06 must revise the flat dark
openings and unfocused gaze seen in pass05, and compare directly with that preserved
face. Technical readability counts are not a substitute for judging the expression.

## Art direction

The pass-04 comparison shows bright, disconnected eye pixels at 32 px/m, pinched lower faces,
thin garment forms beneath substantial heads, and insufficient separation between some hats,
shirts and outer clothes. Preserve the cast's different ages, heights, garments and identities.
Refine the existing forms instead of adding surface noise or increasing overall character scale.

- Design eyes as a small, dark readable cluster with restrained whites. Preserve direction of
  gaze, distinct expressions, staged blinks and speech. A relaxed face should not look vacant or
  exhausted, and a friendly face should not depend on a permanent grin.
- Shape cheeks, jaw and mouth into clear low-poly planes. Keep hair, beards and hats fitted to
  the person; review profiles and oblique views as well as frontal portraits.
- Tailor shoulders, sleeves and garment volumes by preset. Separate dominant cloth areas through
  deliberate values and restrained accent colours. Leave clear negative space between limbs.
- Keep skin/hair identity choices. Clothing contrast is a clothing/material problem. Preserve the
  rig's metre scale, skeletons, physical heights and hand/foot/attachment positions.

## Evidence required

Use the immediately preceding pass 04 as the comparison. Render all ten cast presets, eight
headings, 64 and 32 px/m, plus representative working/seated/mounting poses. Include a live
before/after switch that holds animation time, rotation and density constant. Check the complete
35-animation geometry matrix for invalid coordinates, changed contact anchors or new degeneracy.
Keep the Fisher mixed clothing proof working after the shape/material refinement.

The target is a consistent, appealing cast, not a numerical beauty score. Static art approval and
no-Unity geometry checks do not certify production rendering, actual boat/tool contact or an
"AAA" quality label. Shipping the face and fitted geometry still requires CW-02's real bake,
renderer integration and an in-world comparison at the existing camera scale.

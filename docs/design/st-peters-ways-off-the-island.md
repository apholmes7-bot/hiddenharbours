## What closes each way for the first week

**All normal sea edges.** The merged bands give every edge start/full/limit distances of 130/85/55 m, one standing flag and one lifting flag. Enabling them belongs with the missing first-week progression and must preserve the next-morning distinction above. The west band covers both destinations identified above, including corners. Its dory and fast-hull cases do not prove every hull is contained. Keep entry policy separate from boat capability: a larger hull or an explicit load must not silently grant story progress. A proposed shared Core admission contract would check final crossing, direct load and reactivation, with weather explaining refusal. This is a design proposal, not a second invisible physical barrier. Do not add a drawn wall or move ground to enforce it. Nothing in this audit implements that contract.

**North, south and the unmatched east segment.** There is no new region to load today. Weather can make these boundaries legible, but adding a neighbour is a new way out and must fail the inventory test. The old absence of a scene is not durable campaign protection. PR 1494 covers hull approach, not later destination admission.

**The bar and every on-foot shared edge.** Low tide must remain a physical condition, with the islander's flag an additional first-week condition. PR 1494 does not push walkers, and choosing `PlayersBoat` can even make its weather sampling follow the boat while he is on foot. Use the same story admission decision for a walker, with an authored weather warning and a weather-driven refusal/turn-back before leaving; the exact presentation needs its own review. Merely adding `LandPassageAccess` to the old bar trigger would not close the shared-edge path. Do not pretend that the NPC crossing gate protects the player: it is read by routine routing, not by `RegionTravelCoordinator`.

**The three legacy passages.** Keep them in the audit even while their triggers stand down. Apply the future admission decision to explicit activation and the final loader, so a fallback scene, an opening exception or an interaction cannot bypass it. West Water must remain listed while unbuilt. PR 1494 adds no passage policy. None of these scene objects is edited here.

**Arrival boat and any future ferry.** The present fresh-game journey already loads East Water before landfall. Treat that existing passenger arrival as a narrow, explicit exception if it is retained; it grants no free departure after landing. If the demo must literally load only St Peters, the opening must instead be revised in its own work. Do not infer permission to replay the opening as fast travel. PR 1494 deliberately exempts `OpeningCinematicRunning`; new passenger services would need their own reviewed route and gate.

**Preload and direct scene APIs.** If "closed" means the player cannot visit, parked preloads are not an escape. If it also means no other region may load during the demo, check admission before `Preload` as well. In either interpretation check admission again when a preloaded scene becomes active, since flags and intent can change between warm-up and crossing. The current loader's early reactivation and in-flight-preload branches must not bypass that check. PR 1494 supplies none of these decisions.

**Sleep, Continue and restart.** There is no implemented off-island wake transfer to close on this base. When that subscriber is built, it must check the same story rule before restoring a region. Preserve the original anchor and report a refused destination; do not silently overwrite or migrate the save. A same-region anchor outside a future weather band also needs a deliberate resume policy: weather alone is not proof that a restored player cannot already stand past it. The demo must ship with its intended boot scene and a defined compatibility policy for saves from a later campaign. This audit reads no player save and changes no field or version. PR 1494 does not handle load/continue policy.

**Vehicles and routine crossings.** There is no player driving transfer today. Before adding one, register its route and use the same admission contract. Keep NPC errands a separate policy decision: the first-week restriction on the player does not automatically forbid islanders' routines. The crossing inventory scans both ends so a new reverse-authored link cannot evade review. PR 1494 does not add either behaviour.

**Quest, conversation, ferry, fast travel and rescue additions.** No additional relocation was found in the current call graph. Any future implementation must declare a route and ask the common admission policy, even if it presents itself as a reward, rescue or transport service. Ginny's conversation still needs to record her word, with `st_peters.weather_turned` becoming effective the next morning in the full game; PR 1494 only reads the flag. Day seven is one prerequisite, not a replacement for her word or the other facts. Existing flag storage can represent those decisions without a new saved field; the producer and morning activation are separate work. The demo must retain its boundary after the closing beat.

**Development controls.** Keep direct travel and hull-cycling controls out of the demo input path or behind an explicit development-only enablement. There is no region-warp hotkey to remove on this base; editor scene selection is not ordinary player input. PR 1494's physical tests must not be mistaken for proof that every development hull or direct API call is contained.

# Room fixture contract audit

At `832c6f78f9e607cfbdede4845a3d205551439c91`, a fixture's picture, interaction and saved contents are separate contracts. Cooking stations have working verbs and saved cook jobs, but no occupancy cells in their Defs and no collider in their standalone prefabs. Fridges have a storage Def and a solid cabinet, but no verb, instance store key or transfer binding. Dining rules exist in Core; room furniture does not yet connect seats and tables to those rules. These gaps can mostly be closed using existing save rows. Moving existing contents to different keys or retaining completed food at a station needs a separate persistence decision.

The census finds **24 functional-kind Def assets** (3 cooking, 6 food stores, 5 wash stations, 1 washroom, 7 stowage, 2 freezers), **15 standalone functional fixture prefabs**, and **one dwelling prefab containing 12 relevant fixture components**. Four building prefabs nest another **17** instances of the standalone fixtures. Following those buildings into scenes gives **25 placed cooking/washing/seating/storage components**: **7** in StPeters and **18** in NineMileCreek. Five are cooking stations; two are fridges with no interaction. The separate wardrobe interaction and decorative dining/storage entries are recorded below, as are the dwelling and picnic-table Defs. The camper dwelling prefab is not referenced by a scene. A prefab or a plan row alone is not counted as a placement. The older [building audit](../world/kitchens-bathrooms-audit.md) describes an earlier commit; its zero-kitchen count does not describe this snapshot.

This is a text audit of room cooking, washing, dining and household storage, including their shared galley storage Defs. It also records decorative room furniture and the adjacent picnic-table Def so that they cannot be mistaken for functional dining or storage. Industrial fuel tanks, fish-plant cold stores, cargo holds, carried containers, merchant stock and sleeping fixtures are outside this room-fixture contract. No scene or prefab was opened in the editor, and no asset, code, save or appearance was changed. Unity, gameplay, reachability and visual checks were not run.

## Register

**Reading the tables.** `MISSING` means the contract is absent; `N/A` means that fixture does not need it. All dimensions below are **ground metres**, not occupied grid cells. None of these functional Defs declares occupancy cells, cell size or a cell origin. Sprite-sheet cells are animation/facing frames and must not be treated as floor occupancy. `plan` dimensions come from `RoomFixturePlan.Footprint` or `HomeStoragePlan.Footprint`; `art` dimensions are existing `ApplianceSheets.Foot` values, recorded without judging or changing them. Different frames/sizes are preserved rather than rounded to tiles. A service side inferred from a picture or nearest wall is not an authored interaction constraint.

All food stores use the existing `KitchenStorageStateDto`: `Key`, `StorageDefId`, item/count/freshness stacks, ice expiry and power state. A Def id identifies a **kind**, not a unique container. The default power service is `AlwaysOnKitchenPower`; these bindings alone do not prove a fridge has a working power connection. `KitchenStorageDef` has no food-category whitelist; the future transfer boundary must validate what can be carried into it. Cook jobs save under each placed `_id`; prefab defaults must never be reused as live keys. `RecipeRunner.TryFinish` currently puts the result in `SupplyLocker`, and non-hand inputs are spent from that locker. That is a physical-transfer gap, not evidence that a stove stores groceries.

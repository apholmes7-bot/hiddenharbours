# Goods handling: choices for the owner

These choices are for later review. The design uses the recommendations below; they are not recorded as approvals. The manual-handling rule is already settled.

1. **How much should one hand carry in a pail?** Choose **8 kg including the pail**, **12 kg**, or **always require two hands for a filled pail**. Recommend **8 kg**; heavier loads need a pan or cart. A long fish still has to fit inside.

2. **How should a large fish leave the boat?** Choose **both hands after putting the rod down**, or **first place it in a light pan and carry the pan**. Recommend **both hands** when the fish fits that carry; use a pan or machine for larger ones. Neither choice gives the rod a hidden extra hand.

3. **What happens when a container is full?** Choose **refuse the put**, **spill onto the floor**, or **offer a separate deliberate tip action**. Recommend **refuse** for every normal put; add deliberate pan tipping later. The fish stays in your hand after a refusal.

4. **What can be alive on deck?** Choose **live shellfish with plain movement, fish still**, **all catch visibly alive for a while**, or **no visible life state**. Recommend **live shellfish, fish still**: believable handling without distress or gore. Any later death timer needs species rules.

5. **How should the first sale work?** Choose **one fish set on the counter**, or **a whole pail delivered to a receiving spot**. Recommend **one fish first**, then delivered pails once the loop works. The player keeps the emptied pail; parking a boat or truck never sells its load.

6. **Which small sorting container should come first?** Choose **a 0.65 × 0.45 m low pan**, or **a 0.80 × 0.50 m fish box**. Recommend **the low pan** for the first hand task. The equipment page marks uncertain local sizes for correction before art is made.

7. **Which first cart?** Choose **four-wheel low wharf cart**, or **two-wheel sack truck**. Recommend **the wharf cart**: a visible bed and handle make loading clear; it still cannot carry an oversized tub.

8. **When should powered shore machines arrive?** Choose **after trucks and hand carts work**, or **alongside the first hand cart**. Recommend **afterward**, at the enterprise stage: tractor with a small trailer, then forklift and wharf lifting gear as their loading sites become usable.

9. **How high should small insulated totes stack?** Choose **two total**, **three total**, or **no loaded stacking**. Recommend **two total**, only with compatible locating lids and enough support. Empty nesting is a separate choice for containers built for it.

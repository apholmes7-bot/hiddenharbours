WHAT PASS 9 CHANGES

The body is parametric. Lengths come from age, the height step (one step is one pixel of crown) and the body-shape slider; girth comes from the weight step. The pose library writes body-relative targets through the build, so a grip at the Fisher's belt lands at every build's belt, while world contracts (rail, rung, bench, saddle, wheel) stay in world metres. Walk and run stride with the legs and report their own speed; the engine plays them at moveSpeed / speed_mps.

The look, second pass. Hair styles have silhouettes and keep off the front of the side plane, so E and W show a cheek, an eye and an ear. At the diagonals the far eye is drawn on the near eye's row. Eyes carry a pupil, an iris colour and a white. The yoke slopes into the collar instead of forming a bright shelf.

REPORTED, NOT GATED

World contracts sized for the Fisher. On a build shorter than the Fisher, the default rung, bench, wheel, rail and saddle can be out of reach; the limb clamps and the sidecar reports the shortfall. Cloth. A skirt or apron that meets the ground in a crouch is reported; it folds in the engine. Lying clips on builds taller than the Fisher can reach the cell edge.

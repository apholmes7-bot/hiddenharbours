Prepared from c33f2e47 for the approved Phase B base dcba1a21. The recreation boats land first; sport fishers and the 120 follow in a second intake. Native evidence and the owner’s looks are recorded with the PR.

R1–R3 come from Art desk’s `for-planner/INPUT-2026-10-21-rec-boats-phase-a-rulings.md`; R2 (a) is amended by `for-planner/INPUT-2026-10-22-five-answers-and-rec-boats-r2-again.md`. R4–R5 are the coordinator’s Phase B rulings.

- **R5:** restore registry remains the dory/dory-outboard/punt’s later save item; rooms keep default paints.

**Held:** go-fast cuddy held for kit 10.4's crawl-in and its game intake. The drive's trim, tilt and steer in play: logged, not built. Zero-fit clips/poses and exclusions remain as published.

## Engine compatibility and paints

The reader permits an absent house only on an open hull. Cabin/ship requirements and washboards handling are unchanged. The extractor accepts geometry units exactly m or metres; the walk reader still requires metres. Stored fields are retained. The delivered spec §4.7 says metres, its checker does not check that field, and the house rigs say m: retain the delivered bytes and carry the disagreement to the next spec issue.

Exterior paints share an index only if ramp and offset agree in every published scheme. Single-scheme extraction retains the original path. The three PaintedHull rows use one member-chain filter, in the existing sloop form, without changing ReadMaterialTable or its 16 check. Default exterior group counts are go-fast 11, bowrider 12, cruiser 12; scheme-stable counts are 14, 12, 12. Exterior names are 14 each. Go-fast splits hull/bottom/g3 and deck/g1. Room names are 9/0/11 and default room groups 8/0/11. All fit the ruled limits. All 284 existing generated tables retain identical names, offsets and RGBA bytes. The new rows produce 21 scheme tables.

Room paints stay at their default: IsoFacetHullPresentationService.cs:450–458 reads def.InteriorRamps independently of an exterior scheme. IsoFacetHullRenderer.cs:627–649 creates their textures and :693–705 uploads the separate interior table; IsoFacetHullRegistry.cs:295–301 declares its shader ids. No shader slot count changes.

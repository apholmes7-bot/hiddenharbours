# St Peters key scenes: second issue

Amendment 2 reads the nine re-issued scenes from `HH-st-peters-one-scene-package-2026-10-07` and joins the east end from its file and the Landing's four bait-store companions. This serves P3, the working coast. Stable ids, placed flags, placement selections and retired ids stay unchanged. StPeters.unity is untouched.

## Mounts and ownership

Explicit `host` and `anchor` ids determine the mount; descriptive words still undergo the height check. The eighteen legacy wording rules remain for older packages. Supplied `hostZ` must equal `z`, and `footAt` must equal `foot`. The Head's `form.stp_ne_head` resolves to its existing Form Def.

These stand until a later CD issue agrees. The other six old answers retire from the table.

The east end keeps `keyscene.stp_east_end` and its existing row order. Its seventeen rows join `prop.stp_bait_ice`, `prop.stp_bait_salt`, `prop.stp_bait_bike` and `prop.stp_bait_flag` from the Landing; each id has one home. Its Source names both pinned files. `ReadSecondIssueFromFiles` reads the committed R16 height texture through the game's decode and sampler without an editor. The existing menu entry uses the combined ten-asset import when the east file is present. The village is unchanged.

## Ground and water

The import follows #935 and #951. The loft spur is #935's three-point, 0.9 m path, as recorded in [wave 1b](wave-1b-placement.md). Published still-water support does not itself mean water: physical wetness requires Still > E.

The fall at (106.22, 61.32), Z 2.062, matches the plunge surface within one R16 step and occupies a cell touching the plunge's wet cells. Its own cell may be dry: the origin is the face's foot. The block at Z -0.04 is on pool 01's dry rim, above -0.283. Both moved finds are outside its wet cells. Sweet gale at Z 5.06 is in the fen, 0.19 m below +5.25. These checks pass on the merged maps; no row requires retaining an older value as a failed-check exception.

For the Art desk, stepping stone 3 and marsh marigolds 01/02/03 stand respectively 0.062, 0.432, 0.202 and 0.002 m below the plunge's nominal +2.062. These are notes, not placement acceptance bars. The stone and marigold 03 have dry containing cells; the interpolated ground and water determine wetness at a point. The two finds' origins are about 0.094 and 0.149 m below bilinear ground, although their containing cells meet the water-exclusion bar. Their later placement wave should review that seating.

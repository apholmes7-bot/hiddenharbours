Beards, hair, a bigger wardrobe and a cast of thirty

Rig 10.2 keeps 10.1's body, face and lighting. Beards are rebuilt so each style reads at every facing, and the chin beards stand in front of the collar. Each hair style gets a lit band across the crown and a shape of its own. The wardrobe gains six tops, six outfits, four swimsuits and two bottoms. Twenty NPCs join the cast, and Randomise never dresses a man in a skirt or a dress.

3 · BEARDS · 10.1 AND 10.2
In 10.1 a beard was the skull's two lower rings painted, plus a moustache decal. From the 40° camera the collar covers the chin row, so most of that paint never showed: the goatee drew as a dark ring round the mouth, mutton chops as a moustache, and stubble only darkened fair skin. In 10.2 each style places its pixels separately at each of the eight facings, as the face marks do. The moustache sits on the nose column and one column either side, sideburns on the edge of the face nearest the ear, and a full beard on every column of the cheek the camera sees. The chin beards (chinstrap, goatee, Van Dyke, full and long) have a solid chin that stands in front of the collar. Stubble mixes the skin toward the hair colour, so it shows on any skin.
The Fisher with brown hair, idle, ×5. Left 10.1, right 10.2, each at S, SE and E. Van Dyke and sideburns are new.
4 · HAIR · 10.1 AND 10.2
From the front, most 10.1 hair styles had nearly the same outline, and their differences showed only from behind. 10.2 adds a lit band across the crown and gives each style a shape that reads from the front. The crop has a higher hairline, with the back and sides a tone darker. The mop has three tufts on the crown. The bob and the long have a curtain of hair beside each cheek, set behind the eye so the profile stays clear; the long continues to the shoulders. The bun sits high enough to show above the head. The ponytail is a tied tail that keeps its pixels in profile (10.1's broke into one floating pixel). The buzz cut mixes the hair toward the skin, and the bald head has a highlight.
Ginny without her hood, brown hair, idle, ×4. Left 10.1, right 10.2, each at S, SE, E and N.
5 · NEW CLOTHES
Six tops: a Breton top, hoodie, cardigan, pea coat, life vest and singlet. The Breton's stripes are one pixel row tall on the body and sleeves, so they stay clean at every facing. Six outfits: chest waders, coveralls, a greatcoat with a scarf, wetsuit, sundress and pinafore. Four swimsuits, all barefoot: trunks and briefs for men, a swimsuit and a bikini for women; trunks and briefs on a woman come with a top. Two bottoms: cropped trousers and a long skirt. Each card shows a man and a woman at S in the piece's default colours. Pick one to put it on the wardrobe's build.

All 19 gated checks pass on the Fisher, on each of the twenty new NPCs, and on a bikini, a hoodie and trunks worn by a woman. The checks themselves are unchanged from 10.1.

Character clip proof renders — characterIsoRig6.js rev 6.2, drop of 2026-08-06
==============================================================================

WHAT THESE ARE. Proof renders of the four clips the rev 6.2 drop adds, produced by running
the rig files VERBATIM (eye -> head -> body, the kit's mandatory load order) and blitting
rows = 8 facings x columns = frames. They exist because CI has no graphics device and the
shipped sheets are baked in the editor, so a reviewer would otherwise have nothing to look at.

THEY ARE NOT THE SHIPPED ART. The game sheets are baked by CharacterRigBaker through
ClearScript/V8 inside Unity (Hidden Harbours > Art > Bake Character Sheets - the player), at
the FULL 64x92 cell with no crop and no scaling. These proofs crop each cell to the figure's
own column and scale 4x nearest-neighbour so the pose is legible on a PR page.

HOW TO REGENERATE:   node docs/art/proofs/_characterClipProof.mjs docs/art/rigs <outdir>
Node 22 (V8). No dependencies.

DETERMINISM: two independent runs produced byte-identical PNGs (verified 2026-08-06).

WHY THE PNGs ARE NOT COMMITTED BESIDE THIS LOG. They were rendered and reviewed, and they
belong in LFS like every other .png here -- but the session that produced them had
lfs.github.com blocked by its egress policy, so the objects could not be uploaded and
committing the pointers alone would have left a branch nobody could check out. The
regenerate command above is one line, needs no dependencies and is deterministic, so the
images are a command away rather than lost. Anyone with LFS reachable can run it, drop the
four PNGs into this folder and commit them.

  All ten presets would be 40 sheets / 61.1 MiB, against 2.5 MiB for the whole Iso folder
  today. Fisher-first is the recipe's stated default; adding the cast is one line in

  carry expansion would grow into 4 more sheets (+5.750 MiB). Nothing can carry a pail over a
  rail yet. See CharacterRigBakeMenu.PlayerCarryStanceExclusions -- delete an entry to ship them.

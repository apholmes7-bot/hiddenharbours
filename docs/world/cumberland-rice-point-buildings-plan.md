# Cumberland and Rice Point: buildings to stand and rooms to provide

This is a placement plan, not a placement. It covers every building described in the two regions' data at main `428ab210d78a5f3e65cfac9320c4b130c72847dd`. No code, asset, scene, routine or saved state changes here. The kitchen and bathroom rules come from [draft PR 1275, sections 1.4 and 3](https://github.com/apholmes7-bot/hiddenharbours/pull/1275), inspected at head `9269abfba367e6847f0e01f4e368d82e7924570e`. That draft is a dependency, not evidence that its proposed fixtures or placement step have shipped.

The reasons for enterable rooms follow [CLAUDE.md section 1](../../CLAUDE.md#1-what-youre-building-in-one-breath) and the [canon pillars](../vision-and-pillars.md#3-the-five-pillars): P3 gives people believable homes and workplaces; P2 gives the player tangible property and facilities; P4 requires useful space for goods carried by hand. An exterior landmark alone does not justify a playable room.

## 1. What exists, and what the counts mean

| Region | In source data | Stood shells | Stood interiors | Recommended eventual enclosed interiors | Remaining structures |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Cumberland | **24** | **0** | **0** | **20**: 13 dwellings, 6 civic/trade/service buildings, 1 workshop | 2 barns without an interior use yet; 1 open bar service area; 1 open stage |
| Rice Point | **17** | **0** | **0** | **15**: 11 dwellings, 4 storage/work rooms | 1 barn without an interior use yet; 1 winch housing operated from outside |

These are recommendations across the eventual build-out, **not 35 interiors authorized for immediate construction**. Cumberland's workshop and Rice Point's four storage/work rooms are conditional on the proposed hand-storage use; without those uses the required counts are **19 and 11**. A bathroom rule saying “none” does not forbid an enterable cargo room. The float bar needs cooking stations on its service deck, but does not count as an enclosed interior.

Cumberland's 24 are 22 `lots.*` building records plus the bar and stage in `marina.docks[].parts`. Its marina office and shed are also mentioned in `marina.ashore`; those are references to the same two buildings, not extra buildings. Rice Point's 17 are 12 `lots.*` records, two `wharf.sheds`, two `growth.sheds`, and the winch house named inside `growth.railway`. The last has **no separate building id or footprint**: retain `railway.rpt_s3_railway` as its parent reference until a future content task defines the child. Do not invent a registered identity in this document.

The source plans describe intended buildings. At this baseline, a read-only scan of [Cumberland.unity](../../Assets/_Project/Scenes/Cumberland.unity) and [RicePoint.unity](../../Assets/_Project/Scenes/RicePoint.unity) found zero `BuildingInterior` components, zero `HomeDoor` components, zero prefab instances, no listed structure/shed ids, and no building shells in their named GameObjects. This checks serialized content, not appearance in an editor. Every inventory row below therefore says **none** for what stands, including buildings described as boarded or ruined in the plan.

The [Cumberland RegionDef](../../Assets/_Project/Data/Regions/Cumberland.asset#L15) and [Rice Point RegionDef](../../Assets/_Project/Data/Regions/RicePoint.asset#L15) identify their additive scenes; neither is a building inventory. [CumberlandBuilder](../../Assets/_Project/Code/App/Editor/CumberlandBuilder.cs#L16) calls the land template and routine builder. [RicePointBuilder](../../Assets/_Project/Code/App/Editor/RicePointBuilder.cs#L16) calls the template, door wiring and wreck placement. [LandSceneTemplate.Create](../../Assets/_Project/Code/App/Editor/LandSceneTemplate.cs#L35) creates terrain, water, travel and direct-play support; it does not stand these buildings. Thus **region scene builders exist; regional building-placement passes are missing**.

### Key-scene briefs and phase boundaries

The briefs are embedded in the plans, not separate Cumberland or Rice Point `KeySceneDef` assets. [Cumberland's six briefs](../../Assets/_Project/Data/Terrain/CumberlandGround/plan.json#L2840) cover the marina/bar float, resort from the water, clubhouse, fork/store, Route 91 seam and north beach. The last two add no buildings. [Rice Point's six briefs](../../Assets/_Project/Data/Terrain/RicePointGround/plan.json#L5330) cover the unused wharf, strand crossing, cove/marsh, point ledges, gut and back shore. These anchor the store/head shed, strand house, marsh cottage, point house and camp; the gut adds no building. They are not accepted room layouts.

[Cumberland's design](../design/cumberland.md#saves-and-capabilities-left-to-later-waves) reserves resort guests, marina purchase and charters for M3 and interiors/households for later work. [Rice Point's design](../design/rice-point.md#edges-and-later-capability) reserves ownership, growth, fleet staffing and interiors for M3. Plan those rooms here; stand today's shells first under the appropriate regional placement work. A HomeDef does not override that staging by itself. Rice Point has **11 stage-0 structures** and **6 later additions**: bait shed at stage 2; cold store, three staff/skipper houses and winch house at stage 3. The old store/head shed change use at stage 1 and the existing wharf house becomes a bunkhouse at stage 2; they are not extra buildings.

## 4. What is missing before this can be built

### Regional building placement, not new region scenes

Extend the existing regional workflows with separately owned building/site passes. Neither wrapper currently consumes the building lists. Each pass needs an explicit row mapping **source identity → region → shell/kit family/key → measured room key → scene root → facing/ground position → stage/use**, plus any HomeDef and routine station link. An imported lot is not itself an installed building Def or a builder call. Preserve existing identities; put any genuinely new identity declaration in the additions mechanism, not the frozen identity ledger.

The placement pass must stand/reconcile one site at a time and preserve unrelated regional visuals. It needs a dry run, stable instance ids and a second-run no-change check. A whole-scene CREATE is unsuitable for adding buildings to these existing scenes, and REFRESH currently only preserves/rewires what exists. The proposed passes, their region-specific site rows and their shell/room wiring are **missing work**, not methods this plan pretends are already callable. Only a regional art/placement task may edit the scenes.

### Kits, dimensions and appearance

- House shells and rooms provide reusable families, but the plans' lots are generally larger or differently shaped than the registered village rooms. Record actual shell/room measurements and facing anchors. Preserve the authored lot and access corridor; commission the missing variant rather than rescaling the ground or declaring a mismatch accepted.
- New exact assets/layouts are needed for the bungalow variants, camp, Cape porch, farmhouse ell, regional two-storey stairs/partitions, church, hall, lodge, clubhouse, office/chandlery/shower block, large barns/silo, workshop, storage/cold-room lofts and winch housing. Existing house/shop/wharf/marina rigs reduce source work; they do not remove these requirements.
- The shared interior stander has two families. Wharf industrial rooms, large hospitality/civic buildings and a floating service deck need their own correct integration or an explicit extension. A rig-side room description is not a registered Unity room, collider, door or usable upper level.
- Rice Point's old store, wharf house and head shed must initially show their specified derelict conditions. Restoration variants belong to their stages. No operational light, staffed counter or occupied bunk room is implied by an empty shell.
- Art placement must verify shells against the ground, doors against paths, kitchen/bath partitions against drawn walls/windows, and stairs against both levels. It must retain the support and tide relationship of the bar/stage and their fixtures. Nothing may hang above its floor or float above its supporting deck. This document supplies no visual approval or replacement plate expectation.

### Fixture dependencies and the PR 1275 section 3 join

At the inspected baseline, [wash station Defs](../../Assets/_Project/Data/Resources/WashStations) exist, but `CookingStationFixture`, `WashStationPoint`, `RoomFixturePlan` and `InteriorFixturePlacement` are not on this branch. PRs **1272, 1273 and 1275 were still open drafts** when checked. The generic placement step in PR 1275 is a specification. Its R1 must supply the toilet sit fixture, indoor Shower/Bath, basin reuse, usable kitchen prefabs and the generic planner. PR 1275 reports kitchen prefabs without sprites/colliders and an outdoor wharf shower that is not an indoor shower; do not treat either as final fitted room art.

The two new regional passes should join that same mechanism after it lands:

1. Add one row per fixture using explicit stable `fixture.<region>.<building_key>.<slot>` ids, a declared building root/level, kind, prefab and room-model position. Repeated cottages/houses have separate building keys; never derive identity from the reused kit key or GameObject display name. Use distinct slots for multiple toilets, basins or showers.
2. Add named Cumberland and Rice Point patch steps alongside the spec's St Peters/Nine Mile Creek steps. Use the serialized `BuildingInterior` footprint and `InteriorFootprint.ModelToWorld`; do not copy St Peters coordinates or assume every door lies on the same end. Ground-plan siting and room-local projection are separate frames.
3. Use the proposed `BuildingInteriorStander` post-furnishing hook so rebuilding a supported room restores its fixtures exactly once. For industrial/custom rooms, explicitly join the same table after their own room builder. For F-open, use a supported deck-local frame after the float exists; a land-room transform is not a substitute for a moving deck's support.
4. Validate one `_id` component per fixture prefab, a resolving Def, unique instance ids, row-to-world placement and a second plan with zero operations. Check actual colliders/footprints against walls, furniture, other fixtures and doorway/stair clearance. Bathroom pieces must be enclosed in their washroom; accessible entrances and privacy partitions are part of the layout.

No fixture coordinates can be made final from lot rectangles alone. Do not transplant PR 1275's 1.9 × 2.2 m cottage closet into the bungalow, clubhouse or lodge without a room plan. No save-format change is needed to **plan** or place static room fixtures; future business/growth state is separate work.

### Access, people and working uses

- **Reachability:** the [seam audit](../design/region-seam-audit.md#reachability-from-a-normal-start) records Cumberland's missing incoming/reciprocal travel and Rice Point's shut Route 91 connection. Building work does not fix travel. A placement can be checked by direct Play, but a player-facing property/service cannot be called available until its route works in normal play. Keep the neighbour-scene changes with that scene's owner.
- **Routines:** keep the eight golfer assets and door station intact. Any new meals, washes, jobs or home stops need explicit stations and connected walking lanes, after the rooms stand. Reuse the [washroom routine join](washrooms-plan.md#5-villagers-using-the-washrooms), with the current routine schema when that task starts. No automatic assignment of golfers to empty houses. The rest of Cumberland and all Rice Point households/workers need approved roles and routines.
- **Home listings:** bind the three existing HomeDefs to their exact site keys; stand and verify doors, permitted access, beds and stairs before claiming a listed home is usable. Cross-region arrival/rest must use existing supported behavior, not a workaround or an unrequested save edit.
- **Goods:** for the five conditional work/storage interiors, name the actual storage containers, capacity/data bindings and hand-carry routes when the use is approved. Cargo enters through a reachable door and occupies visible storage; no teleporting goods to an off-screen room. A Def for a truck run is not proof that a building or loading point exists.
- **Business/phase gates:** reserve crew beds, growth-stage buildings, staffed boats, marina purchases, charters and guest operations for their approved phase. The shell order does not unlock any of them. The source's future-stage terrain/footprint measurements are also not permission to grade or re-bake today's ground.

### Acceptance for the later placement work

For each selected building, report the source key, shell stood, room stood or explicit shell-only reason, fixture set, accessible door/levels and affected routines. Verify stable ids and repeatable placement, room/fixture geometry, normal-play reachability where claimed, and any linked HomeDef. Art/scene work then needs an editor inspection and its own accepted visual evidence. Do not change plate baselines merely to make the new result pass. This documentation change is checked by source enumeration, citations and diff scope; it has **no Unity run, gameplay test or visual acceptance claim**.

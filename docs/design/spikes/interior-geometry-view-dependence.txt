READING IT: k=8 faces are pose-invariant and bakeable as-is. Faces at k<8 are either
(a) the same wall CULLED at some facings - bake it, let the shader cut - or
(b) SYNTHESISED per facing (the section lip), which no single mesh can carry.
The two are told apart by whether the k<8 faces' identities also appear at k=8.

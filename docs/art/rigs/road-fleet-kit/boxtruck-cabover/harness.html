Cabover Box Truck — harness

Standalone: no build, no deps, no project. Bakes any facing, paint, preset and pose off the same boxIsoRig.js the engine ships, and asserts the bake against caboverBox.contract.json and the geometry in boxIsoRig.caboverBox.gameplay.json. Served from a folder it also re-hashes the rig against the contract's rigSha256; opened straight off disk the JSON fetches are blocked by the browser and those groups are skipped, not silently passed.

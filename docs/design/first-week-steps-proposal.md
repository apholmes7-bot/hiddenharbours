# First-week steps proposal

Keep the whole first week on St Peters. The player stays with Ginny, earns money from shellfish, learns the rod from shore, buys Basil's broken dory, mends and launches her, and fishes from her. On day 7 or later, five facts make him ready to seek Ginny's word. The full game opens the crossing after the weather turns the following morning; the demo ends at her word and lets him keep fishing on the island.

This is a proposal for the Notebook TASKS page, not a change to the opening. It follows the [first-week canon proposal, PR 1492](https://github.com/apholmes7-bot/hiddenharbours/pull/1492), the owner's island-only direction, and the [five-fact proposal, PR 1511](https://github.com/apholmes7-bot/hiddenharbours/pull/1511). The [current-step characterisation, PR 1499](https://github.com/apholmes7-bot/hiddenharbours/pull/1499) records what the rewrite will replace. Those proposals have not been accepted by this document.

No HUD banners, forced errands, automatic goods transfers or invisible purchase rewards. The notes name a place or a piece of work; the player still carries every purchase in its paper bag and every catch in his hands or a container. Each shelf item can run out independently. The island store has basic fishing supplies and repair materials once chosen; boat hardware belongs in Nine Mile Creek. A missing licence never prevents a cast, dig or trap haul: the unlicensed catch goes back, with the named spoken explanation and no catch-record entry.

## What counts, and what is only guidance

The gate has exactly these five requirements. The suggested teaching order below adds no sixth requirement.

| Requirement | Evidence | Missing TASKS text key in the fact proposal |
|---|---|---|
| The dory is mended | Existing `SaveData.RepairedBoats` contains the configured dory id, presently `boat.dory` | `tasks.st_peters.mend_dory` |
| He holds the cod licence | Existing `SaveData.OwnedLicenses` contains the configured licence id, presently `license.cod` | `tasks.st_peters.cod_licence` |
| He landed a kept fish from the dory | Fact proposal: `st_peters.fact.fish_from_dory` | `tasks.st_peters.fish_from_dory` |
| He sold shellfish | Fact proposal: `st_peters.fact.shellfish_sold` | `tasks.st_peters.sell_shellfish` |
| It is day 7 or later | Restore the Core clock normally, then pass `IGameClock.DayIndex` to `Read(save, dayIndex)`; index 6 begins day 7 | `tasks.st_peters.first_week` |

`IslandWeekRulesDef` in the fact proposal authors the threshold, licence, hull, finfish categories and text keys. `IIslandWeekProgress.Read(SaveData, int dayIndex)` at PR 1511 head `32eb9272b8` returns held facts, ordered missing keys and `AllHold`; it sets nothing. Its caller passes the restored/live Core clock's zero-based absolute day index. It accepts neither seconds nor day length, and has no `ReadSaved` entry point. Do not add another time conversion or use the saved day cache or the day within a season. The separate recorder in that draft writes its two historical keys through the existing `OnboardingFlags` list; that describes the draft, not permission for additional persistence here. Save format remains version 25.

The fish fact means a newly kept finfish while the player occupies the configured dory. A shore fish later carried aboard is not one. A fish released for lack of a licence is not one. A shellfish sale must include an actual shellfish unit sold; selling cod, dumping spoiled clams or merely receiving money is not enough. The existing generic `landed_first_catch` flag cannot prove any of these distinctions.

The ordered notes are guidance. He can buy the rod before selling clams, get his licence before buying the boat, or meet all five facts without taking every lesson. Once all five hold, the next useful note is to see Ginny, even if an optional lesson is unfinished. The player never has to repeat a completed action just to perform it in the suggested order. Actual missing licences, supplies or repair work still matter to their own interactions.

All displayed alternatives fit the existing 31-character step budget. The first week should not appear as eighteen simultaneous chores. Arrival and welcome are context; the book shows the next useful note and retains meaningful completed work. Optional lessons and post-week notes must not become hidden eligibility requirements.

The rod/licence order is deliberate: a new player can get a licence before the shore lesson and retain a permitted catch. This does not forbid fishing earlier. The shore species offered in that lesson must match the accepted licence coverage, which is still a content decision. The notes must never suggest that a bare rod lets him retain every species.

Current source evidence already supplies a clam route: the [Shellfish Licence](../../Assets/_Project/Data/Resources/Catalog/Licenses/ClamLicense.asset) is sold by `seller.leblancs` and permits soft-shell clam, blue mussel and American oyster. Ginny fronts the fee; the licence must still be signed for. The St Peters general-store counter is wired to the island fish buyer. Moving this paperwork to the post office is a future choice, not the present route, and this source trace is not a playthrough.

The logbook/letter remains a separate personal thread. Suggested lines: “Read the letter he left” or “Take a quiet look at the letter”; completion remains `read_logbook`. Its predecessor is reaching the house, its successor is whichever island work remains. He can read it at any point. It is not a sixth gate fact, and the dory must not be described as inherited.

Do not relocate the player or the dory, restart the arrival, remove goods, reset time or charge for the same work. The rewrite is a new reading of saved facts, not a new save format. The entries below describe the proposed note; they do not authorise a migration to grant passage.

The strict five-fact evaluator should remain strict for all saves. A compatibility exception, if accepted, belongs to the separate passage policy and must be recorded explicitly; it must not manufacture a dory catch or shellfish sale. `OwnedBoats`, `ActiveHullId`, `onboarded`, old generic catch totals and old money cannot establish those events.

## What the rewrite still has to build

Main's [QuestBoard](../../Assets/_Project/Code/World/QuestBoard.cs) reads named boolean flags only. It cannot directly interpret repaired-hull lists, licence lists, a clock threshold or the fact evaluator's missing keys. The later rewrite needs a read-only presentation adapter for these predicates, or an accepted extension to the quest condition model. It must keep one source of truth; do not save duplicate “step done” booleans for facts that already have a source. A text key is also not a settable gameplay flag.

The separate fact proposal records only the dory fish and shellfish sale. Any additional history described on this page must remain session-only with a plain snapshot until its persistence step is separately accepted; existing saved evidence may still be read. Reusing the flag list would still add saved meaning and is not authorised by this page. It does not supply exact dig, shore-fishing, first-row, guest-stay, repair-job, word or crossing histories. Proposed keys in this page are suggestions, not registrations. No mandatory saved checkbox should ship for a missing producer. An optional lesson can remain session guidance until its event and persistence rule are ready.

The repair plan needs materials, job order and completion events before it can author the supplies note or the two or three job lines. A future save step would need agreed keys and producers for guest stay, exact digging, shore fishing, repair jobs, rowing, the word and crossing, plus restore semantics and old-save tests. Repair quantities and the pending morning need an authoritative representation before promising reload continuity. Until that step is accepted, keep new progress in plain session snapshots. This page grants no saved field or new flag writer. The physical seller's dory also needs an agreed identity: the fact proposal uses main's `boat.dory`, which does not distinguish the new broken boat from the development dory.

Ginny's conversation and the weather integration own the step from eligibility to word to next morning. The morning rule must cover talking late, sleeping across the day boundary, staying awake, loading before dawn, and reloading after the flag was set. It must remain deterministic from game time and saved facts, with no real-time timer. The demo build must keep passage closed even after the closing conversation; demo-to-full-game continuation is an unresolved choice, not an implemented import or a promise that an isolated save file can be loaded directly. PR 1510 currently shares the full-game save namespace; PR 1518 proposes isolation, but neither provides an explicit transfer path. None of these operations belongs in the read-only evaluator.

Before shipping the rewrite, migrate the pinned old-step tests to the accepted new rules with their reasons, retaining assertions for exact next note, completion source, out-of-order actions and older saves. Recheck the actual catch/sale listeners as well as the pure fact table. Paper-bag carrying, separate shelf stock, repair-by-hand and the weather boundary each need their own integration proof; this document neither changes nor claims visual proof of them.

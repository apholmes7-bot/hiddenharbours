Conventional Box Truck — harness

Standalone: no build, no deps, no project. Bakes any facing, paint, preset, part and pose off the same convBoxIsoRig.js the engine ships, and asserts the bake against convBox.contract.json and the geometry in convBoxIsoRig.convBox.gameplay.json. Served from a folder it also re-hashes the rig against the contract's rigSha256; opened straight off disk the JSON fetches are blocked by the browser and those groups are skipped, not silently passed. Mind the cell: 448 × 352 @ 224,214 — this kit does not use the 384 road cell.

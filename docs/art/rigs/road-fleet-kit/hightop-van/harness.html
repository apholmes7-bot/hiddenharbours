Hightop Van — harness

Standalone: no build, no deps, no project. Bakes any facing, paint, preset, variant and pose off the same vanIsoRig.js the engine ships, and asserts the bake against hightopVan.contract.json and the geometry in vanIsoRig.hightopVan.gameplay.json. Served from a folder it also re-hashes the rig against the contract's rigSha256; opened straight off disk the JSON fetches are blocked by the browser and those groups are skipped, not silently passed.

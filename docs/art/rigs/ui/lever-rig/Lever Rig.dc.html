The squish: in v1 the arm reached ASTERN 73° off the view axis and projected to a third of its length, with nothing to read as depth — the grip tapered to a point, so no cap ever turned toward you, and the hub was a flat decal. All four variants below sit on a higher, more overhead camera, run a shaft 15% longer than v1's, end the grip in a real domed cap, and pivot out of a solid binnacle boss casting onto the console plane.

The catch: seen dead-on, a fore/aft lever projects onto a straight vertical line — so every pixel of downward travel is bought with foreshortening. Pull-down-is-astern and a full-length aft pose are mutually exclusive at yaw 0. 1d and 1a keep the dead-on console and buy back the aft pose with a shorter throw — 1d nearer v1, 1a nearer the fix; 1b and 1c cant the binnacle so the travel goes diagonal and the arm keeps its length.

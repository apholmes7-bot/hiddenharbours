# Goods handling: ruled decisions

**RULED 2026-11-11.** The owner said **"i go with all leans for now"**, accepting the nine recommendations in PR 1042. The [ruling record](../../../backlog/proposed-goods-handling.md#ruled-handling-choices--2026-11-11) carries their scope and the follow-up document audit. All nine choices are resolved below; production contracts, measurements and acceptance remain separate work.

1. **RULED — pail in one hand:** **8 kg gross including the pail**. Heavier loads need an eligible two-hand container or cart. A long fish still has to fit inside.

2. **RULED — large fish leaving the boat:** **both hands after putting the rod down**, when the fish fits the authored carry envelope. Use a compatible pan or machine for larger ones. The rod has no hidden extra hand.

3. **RULED — full container:** **refuse the normal put**. The fish stays in your hand. Deliberate pan tipping is a separate later action with verified room and support.

4. **RULED — landed life treatment:** **live shellfish with plain movement, fish still**, without distress or gore. Any later death timer needs species rules. Presentation remains art-owned; this record changes no animation or asset.

5. **RULED — first sale:** **one fish set on the counter**, then quote and confirmation. Delivered pails follow once the loop works. The player keeps the emptied pail; parking a boat or truck never sells its load.

6. **RULED — first small sorting container:** **a 0.65 × 0.45 m low pan**, measured outside. Depth, clear space and local equipment measurements still need validation before art is made. This short-catch pan is distinct from the long counter receiving pan required for current large cod; the [end-to-end fit gate](04-first-slice.md#the-players-steps) remains.

7. **RULED — first new cart:** **four-wheel low wharf cart**. Its visible bed and handle make manual loading clear; an oversized tub still refuses.

8. **RULED — powered shore machines:** **after trucks and hand carts work**, at the M3 enterprise stage: tractor with a small trailer, then forklift and wharf lifting gear as their loading sites become usable.

9. **RULED — small insulated tote stacks:** **two total including the base**, only with compatible locating lids and enough support. Unmeasured lids grant no stack capability. Empty nesting remains a separate choice for containers built for it.
